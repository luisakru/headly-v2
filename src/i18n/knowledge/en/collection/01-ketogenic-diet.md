# 🥑 Ketogenic diet

Very few carbohydrates, lots of fat: the body then runs on ketone bodies. Small studies show fewer attacks in some people with migraine, often within a few weeks.

- Best done with a doctor or dietitian
- The first days can be hard, the "keto flu" brings tiredness and headache
- Not suitable during pregnancy, with certain kidney or metabolic conditions, or with diabetes medication unless your doctor agrees
