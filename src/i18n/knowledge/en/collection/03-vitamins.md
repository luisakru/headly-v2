# 🥚 Vitamin B12 and B2

There are only a few small studies on vitamin B12. A deficiency can be checked with a blood test, though, and correcting it does no harm.

Vitamin B2 (riboflavin), 400 mg a day, is better studied. It is in the guideline too and turns urine bright yellow, which is harmless.
