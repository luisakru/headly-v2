# 🧠 What is migraine?

Migraine is a neurological disease, not an "ordinary" headache. About one in seven people has it, women about three times as often as men.

An untreated attack lasts 4 to 72 hours. The pain is often one-sided, throbbing and gets worse with movement.

It usually comes with nausea or sensitivity to light and sound. That is why Headly is kept so calm and dark.
