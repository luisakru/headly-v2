# 📞 116 117: Help that comes to you

Sometimes the pain is so bad that you no longer know what to do. And at the same time, getting to the emergency room is unthinkable: bright light, noise, waiting rooms, the drive.

For this, Germany has the out-of-hours medical service on **116 117**. No area code, free of charge and available around the clock.

- On the phone, they work out what you need
- If you can't make it to a practice, a doctor can come **to your home**
- They can give medication you don't have at home, e.g. an injection against pain and nausea
- At night, at weekends and on public holidays, when your practice is closed

If your life is in danger or you have warning signs such as paralysis, trouble speaking or the worst headache of your life, always call **112**.

## My experience

I once had pain so unbearable that I could not possibly have made it to the hospital because of the light and noise. So I called 116 117. I was so, so grateful for that help. Since then I know: I don't have to get through this alone.
