# 💊 Time between doses

Taking medication early usually works best, but doses need time between them. Common values for adults, as stated in the package leaflet:

- Ibuprofen 400 mg: at least 6 hours, at most 1,200 mg a day
- Paracetamol 500 mg: at least 6 hours, at most 4,000 mg a day
- Triptans such as sumatriptan: a second dose no sooner than 2 hours later, only if the migraine comes back

If the first triptan dose does not help at all, a second one in the same attack usually won't either.

Headly saves the time of every intake so you can see the gap. If your doctor prescribed something else, follow their advice.
