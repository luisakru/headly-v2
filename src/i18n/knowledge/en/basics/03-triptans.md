# ⚡ Triptans

Triptans were the first medications developed specifically against migraine. They are not pain relievers in the usual sense: they act exactly where the attack arises.

## How they work

Triptans bind to serotonin receptors on the trigeminal nerve and on the blood vessels of the meninges. There they slow the release of CGRP, calm the irritated nerves and narrow the widened vessels again. More on how an attack unfolds in "What is migraine?".

## Only for migraine

Triptans hardly help against an ordinary tension headache. Besides migraine, they mainly work for the rare cluster headache. So if a triptan reliably helps, that is a hint towards migraine.

But it is not proof. Studies show that headaches with a serious cause can respond to triptans too. A new or unfamiliar headache should therefore always be checked by a doctor, even if the triptan helps.

## Every triptan has its superpower

There are seven triptans in Germany. They differ in how fast they work and how long they stay in the body:

![Half-life of the triptans in hours: sumatriptan and rizatriptan about 2, zolmitriptan 3, almotriptan 3.5, eletriptan 4, naratriptan 6, frovatriptan 26](triptans)

- **Sumatriptan:** the classic, available as a tablet, nasal spray and pre-filled injection. The injection is the fastest of all triptans, often working within 10 minutes, even if you are vomiting
- **Rizatriptan:** the fastest tablet, also as a melt tablet without water. Good for attacks that come on hard and fast
- **Zolmitriptan:** also as a nasal spray, which works through the lining of the nose, handy with nausea
- **Eletriptan:** strong and fairly fast, and the attack comes back less often. Good for severe, long attacks
- **Almotriptan:** known as particularly well tolerated
- **Naratriptan:** gentle, slower to work but lasts longer. Good for moderate, long attacks
- **Frovatriptan:** the marathon runner. Slow to work, but stays in the body for about a day. Often used as short-term prevention for menstrual migraine

In Germany, naratriptan and almotriptan are available in small packs at the pharmacy without a prescription.

In everyday life, the differences are often smaller than the numbers suggest. In the end, only trying shows which one suits you. If one triptan doesn't help, another may still work. Many try two or three until they find the right one. Give each at least two, better three attacks.

## How they work best

- **Take them early,** as soon as the headache starts and is still mild. Later, once even touch hurts, they work much less well
- **Not during the aura,** but only once the headache starts
- **If the migraine comes back,** a second dose is possible after 2 hours at the earliest. If the first didn't help at all, a second usually won't either
- **Combination:** together with naproxen, the attack comes back less often. Discuss this with your doctor
- **On fewer than 10 days a month,** otherwise medication overuse headache may follow, see "Headache from too much medication"

## Side effects

Typical is the "triptan feeling": pressure or tightness in the chest and throat, tingling, warmth, tiredness. It is unpleasant but usually harmless and passes quickly.

Because triptans narrow blood vessels, they are not suitable after a heart attack or stroke, with circulation problems or poorly controlled high blood pressure. Your doctor checks this with you beforehand.
