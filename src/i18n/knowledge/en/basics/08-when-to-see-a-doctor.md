# 🏥 When to see a doctor

Right away, with:

- A sudden, extremely severe headache
- Weakness, trouble speaking or confusion you don't know from your aura
- Fever with a stiff neck
- A headache after a fall or a blow to the head

Soon, if:

- Your headaches change noticeably
- You have more than 3 attacks a month, as prevention may help
- You need medication on more than 10 days a month
