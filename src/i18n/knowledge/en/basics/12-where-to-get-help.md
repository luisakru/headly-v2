# 🧭 Where to get help

Migraine can make you feel alone. It doesn't have to. Here are places I find helpful, mostly in Germany.

## Specialised clinics

- [Schmerzklinik Kiel](https://www.schmerzklinik.de): one of the best-known clinics for migraine and headache in Germany, with information for patients and its own migraine app
- **Charité Berlin:** the Charité's headache outpatient clinic offers specialised diagnosis and treatment, e.g. a migraine week. Best ask directly about current offers
- [DMKG headache centres](https://www.dmkg.de): the German Migraine and Headache Society lists certified centres across Germany

For an appointment you usually need a referral, often from your neurologist. Waiting times can be long, so ask early.

## In an emergency

- **116 117**, the out-of-hours medical service in Germany, can also come to your home, see "116 117: Help that comes to you"
- **112** if your life is in danger or you have warning signs
