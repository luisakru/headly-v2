# 🌘 The phases of an attack

An attack often starts long before the headache and ends a while after it.

- Warning signs: hours to days before, such as tiredness, yawning, food cravings or a stiff neck
- Aura: in about one in four people, mostly flickering or zigzag lines, 5 to 60 minutes
- Headache: the attack itself
- Recovery: often exhausted afterwards, like a hangover

Knowing your warning signs helps you act earlier.
