# 📄 Degree of disability

Severe or chronic migraine can be recognised as a disability in Germany, from a certain degree with a severe disability card. Many people don't know this. The word sounds big, but it can give you real protection in everyday life and at work.

## How migraine is assessed

The pension office (Versorgungsamt) sets a degree of disability (GdB). For migraine there are fixed ranges:

- **Mild course**, attacks about once a month: GdB 0 to 10
- **Moderate course**, more frequent attacks lasting one or more days each: GdB 20 to 40
- **Severe course**, long attacks with strong accompanying symptoms and only a few days' break between them: GdB 50 to 60

From GdB 50 you count as severely disabled and get the card. Other conditions are taken into account too, but the values are not simply added up.

## What it can bring

- **From GdB 20:** a tax allowance
- **From GdB 30:** equal status possible through the employment agency, with better protection against dismissal
- **From GdB 50:** special protection against dismissal, five extra days of leave a year and further compensations

Whether you show the card to your employer is up to you.

## How to go about it

1. Apply at the pension office where you live, often online
2. Add medical reports, especially from your neurologist
3. Add evidence of how often and how severe the attacks are: Headly's PDF report shows the last months day by day

Don't be discouraged if the first decision is lower than expected. An objection is free and often worth it. Advice is available from associations such as VdK or SoVD.
