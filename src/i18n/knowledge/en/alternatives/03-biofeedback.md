# 📈 Biofeedback

With biofeedback, you make processes in your body visible that you normally don't feel: muscle tension, skin temperature, heart rate or how wide a blood vessel at your temple is. A sensor measures, a screen shows it to you as a curve, sound or picture. With some practice, you learn to influence these processes on purpose, and eventually it works without the device too.

![Cycle: body signal, sensor measures, you see it, you learn to steer it](biofeedback)

## What the studies say

Biofeedback is among the best-studied methods without medication and is also in the German guideline. A large analysis of 55 studies found: attacks became clearly less frequent, and the effect lasted months after the training. People also felt less helplessly at the mercy of their migraine. A newer analysis from 2025 confirms that biofeedback reduces how often and how strongly headaches occur.

## The main forms

- **Vasoconstriction training:** you learn to narrow the artery at your temple. Once practised, this can also help during an attack. In studies, this form showed the strongest effects
- **Temperature biofeedback:** you learn to warm your hands. This only works when you are relaxed, and it lowers the stress response
- **Muscle biofeedback (EMG):** you see the tension in your forehead, jaw or neck and learn to release it
- **Heart rate variability (HRV):** you practise calm breathing that makes your heartbeat swing evenly. There are also apps with a chest strap or finger sensor for this

## What to look out for

- **Usually 6 to 15 sessions,** often with psychologists, pain therapists or at headache centres
- **Keep practising at home,** otherwise the effect fades
- **Costs:** German statutory health insurance usually only pays for biofeedback as part of behavioural therapy or in a pain clinic. Ask
- **Keep track:** log the training in Headly as a prevention. As with any prevention, the effect shows after a few weeks

**Our verdict:** well supported, free of side effects and one of the best ways to do something yourself.
