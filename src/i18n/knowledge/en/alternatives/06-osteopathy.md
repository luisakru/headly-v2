# 🤲 Osteopathy

Osteopathy is a hands-on treatment. Osteopaths feel for tension and restricted movement in the body and release them with gentle holds, stretches and pressure, often at the neck, shoulders, jaw, chest and skull. The idea behind it: the body is connected, and a blockage in one place can show up somewhere else.

## What the studies say

There are some small studies, mainly from Italy and the USA. They found fewer migraine days, milder pain and less painkiller use, partly also compared with a sham treatment. Reviews rate the evidence as weak to moderate, though: the studies are small, and a real sham hands-on treatment is hard to design. Those most likely to benefit are probably people whose neck and shoulders are involved.

## What to look out for

- **Good training:** in Germany, "osteopath" is not a protected title. Look for several years of training, e.g. doctors, alternative practitioners or physiotherapists with an extra qualification
- **Gentle on the neck:** fast, jerky manipulation of the cervical spine can, in very rare cases, injure a blood vessel. You can ask for gentle techniques
- **Costs:** many German statutory health insurers subsidise osteopathy, usually with a doctor's referral. Ask yours
- **Keep track:** 3 to 6 sessions are usual. Log osteopathy in Headly as a prevention, then you can see whether something changes
- **New symptoms** such as an unfamiliar aura or paralysis should be checked by a doctor first, see "Warning signs"

**Our verdict:** no replacement for medication, but a gentle addition that does many people good, especially with a tense neck.
