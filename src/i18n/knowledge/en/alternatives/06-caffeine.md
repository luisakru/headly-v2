# ☕ Caffeine

Caffeine is one of the oldest home remedies for migraine. Some drink a strong coffee with the juice of half a lemon right at the start of an attack, others swear by a glass of cola.

## What is behind it

The caffeine is well studied. It makes pain relievers work better, which is why there are combination products of a pain reliever and caffeine, also listed in the German guideline. There are no studies on the lemon itself. Nobody quite knows why it helps, but many swear by it. Cola brings caffeine, sugar and fluid, which helps some people with nausea.

## What to keep in mind

- Drink it early; it is most likely to help at the start of an attack
- Not a lot of caffeine every day: the body gets used to it, and withdrawal can cause headaches itself. Some weekend migraines come from exactly that, when the morning coffee comes later than usual
- If you take a combination product with caffeine, the coffee comes on top

## My experience

Coffee with lemon really helps me sometimes, even though the evidence is thin. And a friend of mine swears by cola. Try what does you good, and check in Headly whether it makes a difference.
