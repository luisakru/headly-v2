# 🥑 Ketogenic diet

On a ketogenic diet you eat very few carbohydrates and a lot of fat. After a few days the body switches over and gets its energy from ketone bodies instead of sugar. This is called ketosis.

## The idea behind it

In his book "Migräne: Heilung ist möglich" (Migraine: a cure is possible), the author Peter Mersch describes migraine as an energy crisis in the brain. Ketone bodies give the brain a second, steadier source of energy. Small studies from Italy show fewer attacks in some people, often within a few weeks. Large, long-term studies are still missing.

## How it works

- Cut carbohydrates sharply, usually to 20 to 50 g a day
- Lots of vegetables, eggs, fish, meat, cheese, nuts, avocado and good oils
- No bread, pasta, rice, potatoes or sugar, and hardly any fruit
- Ketosis can be checked with urine or blood test strips
- Drink enough, use salt and watch your magnesium; this eases the "keto flu" in the first days

## What to watch out for

- Best done with a doctor or dietitian
- Not suitable during pregnancy, with certain kidney or metabolic conditions, or with diabetes medication unless your doctor agrees
- The diet is very restrictive, especially when eating with others

## My experience

When I tried it, I felt I had noticeably less migraine. At the same time it is very restrictive in everyday life. To me it is still one of the most interesting topics around migraine.
