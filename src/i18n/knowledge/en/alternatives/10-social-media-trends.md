# 📱 Home remedies fact-checked

Instagram and TikTok come up with a new migraine trick all the time. Some have a real idea behind them, some are just … creative. Here is our honest fact check, with a wink. And who knows: if it helps you, it helps.

## Migraine glasses

Glasses with slightly reddish lenses that filter certain parts of light. Sounds like a gag but has a real core: small studies found that such lenses can ease light sensitivity, and for some people attacks too. Worth a try for screens and fluorescent light, but not for driving at night.

**Our verdict:** not so crazy after all.

## Acupressure

Two points are shown a lot:

![Two acupressure points: on the back of the hand between thumb and index finger, and on the inner wrist three finger widths below the crease](acupressure)

- **Between thumb and index finger** on the back of the hand, press firmly for one or two minutes. Better not during pregnancy
- **Inside of the wrist,** about three finger widths below the wrist crease. There are hints that this point helps against nausea; travel sickness bands are based on it

**Our verdict:** does no harm, maybe even helps with nausea.

## Peppermint oil

Dabbed on the forehead and temples, it cools pleasantly. It is well studied for tension headache, with small hints for migraine. Keep it away from your eyes and from small children.

**Our verdict:** a true classic.

## Ginger

As powder or tea at the start of an attack. One small study found an effect similar to a triptan, but it is only one study. Against nausea, ginger quite surely helps.

**Our verdict:** tasty and maybe useful.

## Hot foot bath

Feet in hot water, cold pack on the head. The idea: blood is supposed to move from the head to the feet. There are no studies, and migraine probably doesn't work that way.

**Our verdict:** cosy, scientifically more of a spa day.

## A glass of water on your head

Yes, really. We could not find out anywhere why this should help. Maybe it cools a little and makes you sit still, which is something.

**Our verdict:** respect to everyone who tries it. 😄
