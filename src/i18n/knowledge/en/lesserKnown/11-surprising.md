# ✨ Surprising things about migraine

## The chocolate is innocent

Many people think chocolate triggers migraine. Often it is the other way round: craving sweets is one of the early warning signs. The attack has already begun, and the chocolate just happens to be there at the wrong time. The same goes for yawning, needing to pee often or sudden irritability. Knowing these signs lets you act earlier.

## Sex can help

A survey from Münster in 2013 found that for a good half of the people with migraine who had sex during an attack, the pain got better, and for some it went away completely. Probably thanks to the body's own pain relievers such as endorphins. Only if you feel like it, of course.

## You are in good company

- **Hildegard of Bingen** described shining lights and visions in the 12th century that are often read today as migraine aura
- **Charles Darwin, Friedrich Nietzsche and Virginia Woolf** had headaches that by today's view were probably migraine
- **Serena Williams** has spoken openly about her migraine

## It often gets better

For many women, migraine becomes much rarer after menopause, and it eases for many people in older age too. And in pregnancy, many women with migraine without aura have fewer attacks from the second trimester.
