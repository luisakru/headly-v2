# 🥜 Magnesium

The German guideline lists magnesium as an option for prevention, usually about 600 mg a day. The effect is rather small, but it is well tolerated.

- Most common side effect: loose stools; split or lower the dose
- Check with your doctor first if you have kidney disease
- Judge the effect after a few weeks at the earliest
