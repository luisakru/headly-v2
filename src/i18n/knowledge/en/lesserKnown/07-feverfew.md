# 🌼 Feverfew

Feverfew (Tanacetum parthenium) is a medicinal plant from the daisy family. In England it has long been taken against migraine; elsewhere it is sold as a supplement.

## What the studies say

A Cochrane review found only a small effect on average: a little less than one attack per month fewer than with placebo. The studies are small and partly contradict each other. For some people it is worth a try; it is not a miracle cure.

## What to keep in mind

- Not during pregnancy or breastfeeding
- Not if you are allergic to the daisy family, such as chamomile, arnica or marigold
- Chewing fresh leaves can cause mouth sores
- Do not stop suddenly: headaches, joint pain and restlessness can follow, so taper off slowly
- Check with a doctor first if you take blood thinners
- Judge the effect after about three months. Log it in Headly as a prevention
