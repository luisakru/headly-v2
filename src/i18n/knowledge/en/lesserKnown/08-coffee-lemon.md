# ☕ Coffee with lemon

A home remedy you hear about again and again: a strong coffee with the juice of half a lemon, drunk right at the start of an attack. Others swear by a glass of cola.

## What is behind it

The caffeine is well studied. It makes pain relievers work better, which is why there are combination products of a pain reliever and caffeine, also listed in the German guideline. There are no studies on the lemon itself; perhaps it simply makes the coffee easier on the stomach. Cola brings caffeine, sugar and fluid, which helps some people with nausea.

## What to keep in mind

- Drink it early; it is most likely to help at the start of an attack
- Not a lot of caffeine every day: the body gets used to it, and withdrawal can cause headaches itself. Some weekend migraines come from exactly that, when the morning coffee comes later than usual
- If you take a combination product with caffeine, the coffee comes on top

## My experience

Coffee with lemon really helps me sometimes, even though the evidence is thin. And a friend of mine swears by cola. Try what does you good, and check in Headly whether it makes a difference.
