# 💊 Magnesium, B2 and Q10

Three supplements that are often mentioned together. All three play a part in the energy metabolism of nerve cells, the same idea as behind the ketogenic diet. On their own, their effect is rather small, but they are well tolerated.

## On their own

- **Magnesium:** about 600 mg a day. Listed in the German guideline. The most common side effect is loose stools; then split or lower the dose. Check with a doctor first if you have kidney disease
- **Vitamin B2 (riboflavin):** 400 mg a day. Also in the guideline. It turns urine bright yellow, which is harmless
- **Coenzyme Q10:** in studies usually 100 mg three times a day. The evidence is thinner than for the other two

## Together

There are combination products with all three. A study with one of them found mainly milder pain; fewer migraine days were not clearly shown. Whether the combination does more than the single supplements has not been studied well.

And vitamin B12? There are only a few small studies. A deficiency can be checked with a blood test, though, and correcting it does no harm.

## What to keep in mind

- Judge the effect after two to three months at the earliest; that is what your entries in Headly are for. Log it as a prevention, and Headly compares before and after
- Combination products are often more expensive than the single supplements; the daily dose is worth a look
- Talk to your doctor if you take other medication
