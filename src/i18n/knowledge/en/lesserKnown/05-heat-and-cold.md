# 🧊 Heat and cold

Cold on the forehead, temples or neck eases the pain for many people. Cold narrows the blood vessels and calms the nerves, and small studies show an effect.

- A cold pack or cooling pad, always wrapped in a cloth, never directly on the skin
- 15 to 20 minutes, then a break
- Peppermint oil on the forehead and temples also cools; keep it away from your eyes

Warmth helps more when your neck and shoulders are tense, for example a heat pack or a warm shower.

Some people like both at once: a cool head and warm feet. Try what feels good and make a note of it.
