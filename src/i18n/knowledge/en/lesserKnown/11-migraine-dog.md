# 🐕 Migraine dog

Many people with migraine say their dog behaves differently before an attack: clingier, restless or more watchful.

In a US survey, about half of the dog owners had noticed something like this. Specially trained alert dogs have hardly been studied.

Still, a dog can be a great comfort during an attack.
