# My collection

Not medical advice. This is what I have read and tried over the years. What helps me may not help you.

## Ketogenic diet

A ketogenic diet is very low in carbohydrates and high in fat, so the body runs on ketone bodies. Small studies show fewer attacks in some people with migraine, often within a few weeks. Large, long-term studies are still missing.

- Best done with a doctor or dietitian
- The first days can be hard, the "keto flu" brings tiredness and headache
- Not suitable during pregnancy, with certain kidney or metabolic conditions, or with diabetes medication unless your doctor agrees

- [Studies on ketogenic diet and migraine (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=ketogenic+diet+migraine)

## Magnesium

The German guideline lists magnesium as an option for prevention, usually about 600 mg a day. The effect is rather small, but it is well tolerated.

- Most common side effect: loose stools or diarrhoea; split or lower the dose
- Check with your doctor first if you have kidney disease
- Judge the effect after a few weeks at the earliest

- [German migraine guideline (AWMF)](https://register.awmf.org/de/leitlinien/detail/030-057)

## Vitamin B12 and B2

There are only a few small studies on vitamin B12. A deficiency can be checked with a blood test, though, and correcting it does no harm. Vitamin B2 (riboflavin), 400 mg a day, is better studied. It is also in the guideline, but turns urine bright yellow.

- [Studies on vitamin B12 and migraine (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=vitamin+b12+migraine)
- [Studies on riboflavin and migraine (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=riboflavin+migraine)

## Migraine dog

Many people with migraine say their dog behaves differently before an attack: clingier, restless or more watchful. In a US survey, about half of the dog owners had noticed something like this. Specially trained migraine alert dogs have hardly been studied. Still, a dog can be a great comfort during an attack.

- [Study on dogs and migraine (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=migraine+dogs+Marcus+Bhowmick)
