## 2. Principle: your data stays on your device

Headly stores your entries only in your browser on this device. Your entries are not sent to external servers, third parties or the developer. There is no cloud sync, no user account and no registration.

## 3. Hosting

The app is served as a web app by Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the app, the provider processes technically necessary data in its server logs: IP address, date and time, requested file and browser identifier (user agent). Your health entries are not part of this.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the app securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). A data processing agreement is in place with the provider: https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which ensures an adequate level of data protection for transfers to the USA.

## 4. What data is stored?

Headly only stores data that you enter yourself:

- Date and time of migraine attacks
- Pain level (scale 0–10)
- Medications taken and their doses
- How well medications worked
- Prevention measures and their periods

Under Art. 9 GDPR, this data is a special category of personal data (health data). It is processed only locally on your device, based on your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).

## 5. Purpose of processing

The recorded data is used only for:

- personal record-keeping of migraine attacks
- supporting doctor visits with structured overviews
- analysing patterns and treatment progress for personal use

## 6. Sharing with third parties

Your entries are not shared with third parties. They only leave your device if you actively use the export function and pass on the exported file yourself. For the hosting provider's server logs, see section 3.

## 7. Export and import

Headly lets you back up your data as a JSON file. The file is created only on your device. You decide where and how to store or share it. You can restore a backup with the import function.

## 8. Deleting data

You can delete all data stored in Headly at any time under Profile → Data → "Delete all data". After deletion, the data cannot be recovered. Clearing your browser's website data also removes your entries, so back them up first if you want to keep them.

## 9. Your rights under the GDPR

- Access (Art. 15): all your entries can be viewed in the app at any time.
- Rectification (Art. 16): you can edit all entries at any time.
- Erasure (Art. 17): you can delete single entries or all data.
- Data portability (Art. 20): export as JSON at any time.
- Objection (Art. 21): you can object to processing based on legitimate interests (section 3).
- Complaint (Art. 77): you can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

## 10. Tracking and third parties

Headly uses no tracking, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. Fonts and icons are part of the app and are not loaded from other servers.

## 11. Security

Your entries are kept in your browser's storage and protected by your device's security features, such as the screen lock and device encryption. The developer never has access to your entries.

## 12. Children

## 13. Medical note
